>>> Which number is bigger: 9.9 or 9.11?
9.  9 is bigger than 9.11.

Here's how to compare them:

*   **9.9** - has a 9 in the tenths place and a 9 in the hundredths place.
*   **9.11** - has a 9 in the tenths place and a 1 in the hundredths place.

Since 9 is greater than 1, 9.9 is the larger number.
